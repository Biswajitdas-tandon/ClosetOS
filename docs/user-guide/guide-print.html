Getting started

You'll receive an email address and password from whoever set up your account. There's nothing to install.

  1. Open closetos-iota.vercel.app/login on any phone or computer.
  2. Enter your email and password → Sign in.
  3. First time in, you'll see a short welcome with three suggested steps: add an item, compose an outfit, plan a trip. Start with the first.
Navigation. The top bar has Library · Outfits · Calendar · Wishlist · Packing · Add item, and your email on the right opens Account. The front page (the ClosetOS logo) shows sample pieces as a preview — those are examples, not your items. Your items are in Library.

Library

Every item you own, in one grid. Photo first, details behind it.

Add an item

  1. Add item in the top bar.
  2. Click the square to add a photo (or drop one in). One photo per item.
  3. Pick a category — Apparel, Accessories, Jewelry, Silver, Artwork. The fields below change to fit it (see What you can record).
  4. Fill in what you know. Only artwork needs a title; everything else is optional.
  5. Save item. You land on the item's page.

Find things

  • Category chips at the top of Library filter to one category; each chip shows its count.
  • Search matches title, brand, colour, material and notes — e.g. navy linen.

An item's page

  • Edit — change any detail, replace the photo, or set the status.
  • Share — a view-only link for this item (see Sharing).
  • Delete item at the bottom — asks you to confirm; removes the item, its photo, and takes it out of any outfit or packing list.
  • Last worn appears automatically once the item has been in a dated outfit.

Status

Set on Edit. Useful for pieces that are out of the house without deleting their record.

What you can record

Every item has the same base — brand, colour, material, price, purchase date, notes — plus fields specific to its category.

CategoryExtra fields
ApparelSize · Care instructions
AccessoriesSize
JewelryWeight (g) · Hallmark
SilverWeight (g)
ArtworkTitle (required) · Type (painting, sculpture, print, photograph, digital, mixed media) · Medium · Artist · Artist bio · Date created · Date acquired · Purchase price · Current estimated value · Location (room / storage) · Provenance · Insurance status and value · Condition (mint → poor)

Prices are recorded in INR. Leave any field blank rather than guessing — you can always come back with Edit.

Outfits

Group items into a look, and optionally record the day you wore it.

  1. Outfits → New outfit.
  2. Give it a name (e.g. Diwali brunch), an occasion, and notes if you like.
  3. Tap items to add them — your whole library is offered, grouped by category.
  4. Date worn (optional). Add a date and two things happen: the outfit appears on that day in Calendar, and each apparel or accessory item in it gets that date as Last worn.
  5. Save outfit.

An outfit's page shows its items with photos; each one links back to the item. Outfits can be shared like items.

Calendar

A month at a glance: which outfits you wore, and when.

  • Use the ← → arrows to move between months. Days with a logged outfit show it as a pill.
  • Click a day to open it. From an empty day, Compose outfit for this day opens the outfit composer with the date already filled in.
  • A day's view lists every outfit logged that day and the items in each.

Packing

Plan a trip, get suggestions from your own wardrobe based on the forecast, then tick things off as you pack.

  1. Packing → New trip.
  2. Trip name, destination (a city or place), start and end dates, and a trip type: leisure, beach, work, wedding, travel, evening, formal.
  3. Get suggestions from your library. ClosetOS looks up the destination, pulls the forecast for your dates, and picks apparel and accessories from your Library — showing the expected highs, lows and chance of rain, and how many items it matched.
  4. Save trip. The suggested items become the trip's checklist.
  5. On the trip's page, items are grouped by category with a checkbox each. Tick as you pack; the trips list shows how far along you are.
Forecast range. Live weather is available about two weeks ahead. For trips further out, suggestions use the season for that month instead of a forecast.

Wishlist

Things you're considering, kept separate from what you own.

  • Wishlist → Add to wishlist: title, a link to where it's sold, an image link, category, current price and your target price, notes.
  • The wishlist grid shows the image, both prices and a link out to the source.

Sharing

Show one item or one outfit to someone without giving them your login.

  1. On the item or outfit page, Share.
  2. Choose how long the link lasts: 7 days, 30 days, or never expires.
  3. Create share link → Copy and send it however you like.

Whoever opens it sees a read-only page with the photo and details — nothing else from your library. Links can't be switched off from the app yet, so prefer 7 or 30 days over never unless you mean it.

Your data

It's yours. Account (your email, top right) shows your item and outfit counts and two actions.

  • Download export (.zip) — every item, outfit, calendar entry, wishlist and trip as data files, plus your original photos.
  • Delete account — permanently removes everything, including photos. You're asked to confirm; there's no undo.
